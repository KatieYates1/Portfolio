Subliminal

For Subliminal, I did most of the scripting in Unity working with Unity’s VR asset items. For the main gameplay I worked on walking around the planet, traversing to different scenes, animation, creating 3D assets and textures, working with Unity’s VR headset viewing situation, and adding everything into all the Unity scenes. This game was made for mine and my teammates XR asset Production class. As a team of two we used Ink to add interactive narrative gameplay aspects to really bring the game to life. As you traverse the planet, you talk to the Evil Eye Cat and three more creatures while taking in your changing surroundings, in the end you have to make a decision that affects the entire planet.Subliminal
